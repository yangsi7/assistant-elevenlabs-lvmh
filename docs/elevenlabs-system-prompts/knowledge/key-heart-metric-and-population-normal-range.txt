ummary: Key Heart Metrics & Normal Ranges**

---

### 1. **Daily Heart Rate**

- **Resting HR (RHR):** ~60–100 bpm for healthy adults. <60 can be normal for athletes. >100 is tachycardic.

- *General Population Avg:* ~70–80 bpm

- *Interpretation:* Lower RHR (50–60 bpm) often implies better fitness. Persistent elevation (>100) or consistently low (<40) may need evaluation.

- **Maximum HR:** Typically **220 – age** during max exertion.

- *Interpretation:* Spikes during daily routine (e.g., >150 bpm without exercise) can indicate stress/anxiety or arrhythmia.

- **Minimum HR:** ~50–60 bpm (often during sleep).

- *Interpretation:* <40 bpm (unless athletic) could suggest bradycardia or conduction issue.

---

### 2. **Atrial Fibrillation (AFib) Metrics**

- **AFib Burden (% of day in AFib):**

- *Normal (No AFib):* 0%

- *Low Burden:* <1–5%

- *Moderate:* 5–50%

- *High:* >50% (often persistent/permanent)

- **Longest AFib Event:**

- *Normal:* 0 if no AFib. Any episode >30s is clinically relevant. >48h increases stroke risk.

- **Highest HR in AFib:**

- *Controlled AFib:* <110 bpm at rest.

- *Rapid AFib:* >140–150 bpm at rest can cause symptoms.

---

### 3. **AV (Atrioventricular) Block**

- **First-Degree:** Prolonged PR (>200ms) but no missed beats; often no symptoms.

- **Second-Degree:** Some atrial beats not conducted;

- *Mobitz I (Wenckebach):* May be benign.

- *Mobitz II:* More serious; often pacemaker needed.

- **Third-Degree:** Complete dissociation between atria & ventricles; definite pacemaker indication.

---

### 4. **Pauses > 3 Seconds**

- *Normal:* 0 while awake; rare or none >3s during sleep.

- *Frequent/Prolonged Pauses:* Suggest sick sinus or advanced AV block; can cause dizziness/syncope.

---

### 5. **Symptom Relevance**

- **Palpitations, Dizziness, Chest Pain, Fainting** → Evaluate.

- **Bradycardia (<40 bpm) + Symptoms** → Possible conduction issue.

- **Tachycardia (>100 bpm rest)** → Could be AFib, SVT, or stress.

- **AFib + High HR** → Risk for complications (stroke, heart failure).

- **Pauses** → Syncope risk if frequent.

---

### 6. **Comparisons to General Population Norms**

- **Resting HR:** Most adults ~70–80 bpm; athletes may be lower.

- **AFib:** Affects ~1–2% overall, increasing with age (~>10% in 80+); 0% burden is “normal.”

- **AV Block:** 1st-degree common with age/meds; 2nd/3rd-degree less common, but serious.

- **Pauses:** Rare in healthy individuals, mostly 0. Small % of healthy people might have occasional 2s pause in sleep, but >3s is unusual.

---

**Note:** Any concerning trends (e.g., steadily rising resting HR, large AFib burden, second-degree or higher AV block, frequent pauses) should prompt medical consultation.
